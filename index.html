Baizora Score — Use It as a Filter

Switch to the Scores tab and sort by Baizora — a composite of RS, Momentum, Breakout, Trend, and Volume Pressure. Stocks scoring 70+ are broadly strong across all signals; below 30 are broadly weak. Cross-reference individual scores to understand why: high RS + high Breakout means sustained multi-week strength confirmed by today's price and volume action — a powerful pre-filter before deeper analysis.

⟁

Volume Spike Breakout

Sort 1D V CHG% highest to lowest. Volume surging 100%+ alongside a rising price is a classic breakout signal. Volume surging with a flat price — quiet accumulation — is a different pattern worth tracking. Both start with the same sort; the price column tells the story.

◷

Multi-Week Momentum

Switch to the 3M or 6M window and sort by P CHG%. This surfaces stocks in a sustained uptrend — not just a one-day pop — a useful filter for identifying sustained moves in the data.

Index Rebalancing Tracker

Check the Index Membership tab for pending S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 additions. Stocks about to join a major index get systematically bought by index funds ahead of the rebalance date — a well-documented, news-driven pattern.

📈 Reading the Sparkline

Scan the 1Y TREND column for sparklines with a clear upward slope and a volume marker (▲) near the right edge. That combination — a rising trend backed by a recent high-volume event — is a data point worth examining further. The closer the marker to today, the more recent the trading activity.
