Baizora Score — Use It as a Filter

Switch to the Scores tab and sort by Baizora — a composite of RS, Momentum, Breakout, Trend, and Volume Pressure. Stocks scoring 70+ are broadly strong across all signals; below 30 are broadly weak. Cross-reference individual scores to understand why: high RS + high Breakout means sustained multi-week strength confirmed by today's price and volume action — a powerful pre-filter before deeper analysis.

See the historical backtest →

⟁

Volume Spike Breakout

Sort 1D V CHG% highest to lowest. Volume surging 100%+ alongside a rising price is a classic breakout signal. Volume surging with a flat price — quiet accumulation — is a different pattern worth tracking. Both start with the same sort; the price column tells the story.

◷

Trading Near SMA200

Switch to the TECH tab and sort by Dist SMA200 — the closer to zero, the nearer price is trading to its 200-day moving average, a level long-term investors watch as key support. A stock pulling back toward that line while still holding above it can be a healthy setup worth watching for a bounce.

Index Rebalancing Tracker

Check the Index Membership tab for pending S&P 500 or Nasdaq-100 additions. Stocks about to join a major index get systematically bought by index funds ahead of the rebalance date — a well-documented, news-driven pattern.

📈 Reading the Sparkline

Look for sparklines where both markers appear green and close together — the ▲ triangle marks the highest-volume day of the year, the ● circle marks the largest single-day price move. When both are green and recent, they reflect institutional buying activity that has historically preceded strong advances.

Quick Answers
Common questions — click to expand
Is Baizora free? +
Yes — the full Baizora platform is currently free to use for every signed-in user, no subscription required. Create a free account or sign in to get started.
Why Baizora? +
Focused, not overwhelming: S&P 500 & Nasdaq-100 stocks well represent the broader market, not thousands of tickers to sift through.
All timeframes at once: 1D to 1Y in one row, no chart-switching.
Data, not opinions: objective statistics, no buy/sell calls, no ads.
Instant scanning: the whole index in seconds, with easy sorting on any metric.
Unique Baizora Score: combines five signals into one filter.
Visual patterns: 1-year sparklines flag the biggest price & volume days.
Reliable fundamentals: sourced directly from SEC filings.
Index news tracked: S&P 500 & Nasdaq-100 membership changes, updated daily.
Free to try: explore the whole universe with our Free Tier, no login required.
How we compare: an honest look at Baizora vs. Finviz and TradingView, including where they're the better fit.
Why is signing in needed? +
Signing in unlocks the full 500+ stock universe, syncs your watchlist across devices, tracks membership-change history for your account, and helps us prevent abuse of the platform — the Preview (top 20 movers per index) still needs no login at all. Creating an account is free.
How does the 7-day free trial work? +
New subscribers get 7 full days of unrestricted access before any charge. Cancel anytime during the trial at no cost. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.
When does today's data update? +
Dashboard data updates after US market close, typically 6–7 PM ET on weekdays. Market news refreshes daily around midday ET, plus again after the close on weekdays. The daily news video lands around 2:30 PM ET. Chart highlights update around 6 PM ET on weekdays and around 10 AM ET on weekends.
Why doesn't Baizora show live intraday prices? +
We tested it: across the 500+ stocks in the two indices we track (S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100) and 130,000+ trading days, whether a stock was up or down mid-day barely moved the odds of it closing higher the next day (about 51% either way), and the difference in next-day return was too small to trade around. Since intraday ticks don't carry meaningful predictive value, Baizora shows end-of-day close prices — the same close-to-close trend data our metrics are built on.
Is Baizora a financial advisor? +
No. Baizora surfaces structured data and statistical signals only — not investment, trading, or financial advice.
How do I contact support? +
We look forward to hearing from you, and welcome all suggestions and feedback. Email support@baizora.com and we'll respond as soon as possible, typically the same day. Or click Ask in the bottom-right corner for our AI assistant, available 24/7.
