What Baizora is

Baizora is a free, convenient scanning tool built around two indexes — the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 — not the whole market. That narrower scope is deliberate: index membership is itself a quality filter, so every ticker tracked is already large-cap and liquid. Baizora isn't built for day traders — it's built for people who care about the trend, not the tick. Data updates once daily after market close, by design — that avoids intraday noise, and end-of-day volume reflects a true full-session total rather than a partial intraday figure, which matters since volume is one of Baizora's core signals.

Philosophy

The goal is not to predict the market — it's to help identify areas of increased attention and activity using transparent, structured signals rather than black-box models. Every output is grounded in consistent statistical comparisons over short-term windows.

Current Status

Baizora is actively evolving — new scores, data sources, and tools are added regularly based on user feedback and real-world usage.

Principles

Built with a focus on clarity, simplicity, and data-driven logic. No recommendations. No predictions. Just structured data — presented consistently, updated daily, and designed to be understood at a glance.

About the Founder

Baizora is built by Matthew Duan, a trader with over 20 years of hands-on experience in U.S. equity markets, backed by a graduate background in statistics with a focus on financial data analysis. Baizora reflects both: two decades of practical market judgment, paired with the discipline of rigorous statistical methods.

I built it because I wanted a tool that does one thing well — surface unusual price and volume activity across the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, using transparent, rules-based logic instead of black-box predictions or hype.
