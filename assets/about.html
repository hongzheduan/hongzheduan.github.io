About Baizora

Baizora is a simple, rules-based market scanning system focused on US equities. It analyzes price and volume data to highlight unusual activity in large-cap stocks.

The goal is not to predict the market, but to help identify areas of increased attention and activity, using transparent and structured signals rather than black-box models.

The system is currently in early development and will continue to evolve based on user feedback and real-world usage.

Built with a focus on clarity, simplicity, and data-driven logic.
