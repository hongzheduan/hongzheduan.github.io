Baizora vs. Finviz vs. TradingView

Honest Comparison · Not a "Why We're Better" List

Finviz and TradingView are both excellent, widely-used tools — this page isn't trying to talk you out of them. Baizora does one narrower thing: daily price & volume signal scanning across the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, with a composite score and index-membership tracking neither of the others offer. If you need live intraday charting, options screening, or the whole market, they're the better fit — that's covered below too.

Which Fits You

Baizora is a good fit if you...

  • Only care about S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100 stocks and don't want to filter out the rest of the market yourself
  • Do research after market close and want to quickly scan through every ticker, not live intraday trading
  • Want a composite score instead of building your own multi-filter screen from scratch
  • Want to know when a stock is about to join or leave a major index
  • Don't want ads or upsell prompts while you're looking at data

Finviz or TradingView is a better fit if you...

  • Need live intraday / streaming prices for day trading
  • Trade options, crypto, forex, or stocks outside the S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100
  • Want extensive manual charting tools and community-built indicators
