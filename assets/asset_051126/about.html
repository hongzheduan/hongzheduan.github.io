Market Data Intelligence · US Equities
What Baizora is

Baizora is a simple, rules-based market scanning system focused on US equities. It analyzes price and volume data to highlight unusual activity across large-cap stocks in the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100.

Philosophy

The goal is not to predict the market — it's to help identify areas of increased attention and activity using transparent, structured signals rather than black-box models. Every output is grounded in consistent statistical comparisons over short-term windows.

Current Status

The system is currently in early development and will continue to evolve based on user feedback and real-world usage. We're in a limited early access phase — your input directly shapes what gets built next.

Principles

Built with a focus on clarity, simplicity, and data-driven logic. No recommendations. No predictions. Just structured data — presented consistently, updated daily, and designed to be understood at a glance.
