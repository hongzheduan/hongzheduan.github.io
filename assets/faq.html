Frequently Asked Questions

Below are answers to the most common questions about Baizora, including preview access, subscription, and account management.
1. How does the free preview work?
Baizora provides a free preview experience that does not require login or account creation. Users can immediately access a limited set of market data to understand how the system works. The preview is designed to help users evaluate data quality, structure, and platform usability before committing to a subscription.
2. Do I need to sign up to use Baizora?
No sign-up is required for the free preview. You can access selected market insights directly. However, creating an account is required if you want to access full features, save preferences, or manage a subscription plan.
3. How does the 7-day free trial work?
The 7-day free trial provides full access to Baizora’s platform features, including complete market data views and analysis tools. During the trial period, you can explore all functionality without limitations. You may cancel at any time via the Stripe Customer Portal, and no charges will be applied.
4. How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
All subscription actions (upgrade, downgrade, cancel, billing) are handled securely through the Stripe Customer Portal. Click the “Manage Account” button above to open your portal directly. Changes apply immediately or at the end of your billing cycle depending on the action.
5. How is the data updated?
Market data is processed and updated daily after U.S. market close, typically between 5:00–6:00 PM Eastern Time. This ensures consistent and stable daily comparisons without intraday noise.
6. How can I contact support?
If you have any questions or encounter issues, you can contact us at support@baizora.com. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
