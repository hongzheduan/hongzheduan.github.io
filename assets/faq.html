Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Baizora — how the platform works, how to manage your subscription, and how to get the most out of the data.

Getting Started
How does the free preview work? +
Baizora provides a free preview that requires no login or account creation. You can immediately access a curated set of market signals — the top 3 stocks by daily price increase — to evaluate data quality, structure, and platform usability before committing to a subscription.

The preview includes live sparkline charts, multi-timeframe rolling statistics, and fundamental data (PE, market cap, EPS, sector). It's a genuine taste of the full product, not a stripped-down demo.
Do I need to sign up to use Baizora? +
No sign-up is required for the free preview. You can access selected market insights directly at baizora.com/free.

A paid account is required to unlock the full dashboard — all 500+ S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 symbols, all timeframes (2W to 1Y), sortable columns, and full fundamentals.
How does the 7-day free trial work? +
When you subscribe, you get 7 full days of unrestricted access to the complete platform. No limitations, no partial data.

You may cancel at any time during the trial via the Stripe Customer Portal and no charges will be applied. After 7 days, your subscription begins automatically at $9.99/month or $99/year depending on the plan you selected.
✓ No credit card charged during trial
Platform & Data
How is the data updated? +
Market data is processed and updated daily after U.S. market close, typically between 5:00–6:00 PM Eastern Time. This ensures consistent, stable daily comparisons without intraday noise.

All signals — price changes, volume behavior, moving average ratios, sparklines, and fundamentals — reflect the most recent session's closing data.
What do the sparkline markers mean? +
Every stock includes a 1-year price trend sparkline with two visual event markers:

▲ Triangle — marks the highest-volume trading day of the past year. Green if price was up on that day, red if price was down.

● Circle (with glow) — marks the highest single-day price change of the past year. Green if volume was elevated on that day, red if volume was low.

These markers let you instantly spot when unusual activity occurred — without scanning tables. Hover the "1Y Trend" column header in the dashboard for a reminder.
What do the timeframe windows show? +
Baizora supports six rolling timeframes: 2W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 9M, and 1Y, plus a dedicated 1D view.

The 1D view shows today's price change, volume change, and their ratios vs. 21-day moving averages — a quick daily snapshot.

All other windows show the total price change over that period plus the single largest price and volume moves within the window and when they occurred.

Hover any column header in the dashboard to see its exact definition.
What stocks does Baizora cover? +
Baizora covers all current constituents of the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 — approximately 500+ large-cap US equities with consistent liquidity and institutional-grade data quality.

The platform focuses exclusively on large-cap stocks to ensure data consistency and reliability across all signals.
Is Baizora a financial advisor or recommendation service? +
No. Baizora is a data intelligence platform — it surfaces structured statistical signals, not investment recommendations. Nothing on this platform constitutes financial, investment, trading, or professional advice.

All signals are generated by automated systems and should not be interpreted as buy, sell, or hold recommendations. Always conduct your own research or consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
Subscription & Billing
How do I manage or cancel my subscription? +
All subscription actions — upgrade, downgrade, cancel, and billing history — are handled securely through the Stripe Customer Portal.

Click the "Manage Account" button in the header above, or log in and use the Account menu on the homepage. Changes apply at the end of your current billing cycle. Cancellations during the free trial incur no charges.
What's the difference between monthly and yearly plans? +
Both plans include identical features — full access to all symbols, timeframes, sparklines, and fundamentals.

Monthly: $9.99/month — flexible, cancel anytime.
Yearly: $99/year — approximately $8.25/month, saving ~17% vs monthly billing.

You can switch between plans at any time via the Stripe Customer Portal.
What happens after the free trial ends? +
After 7 days, your subscription automatically continues at the rate of the plan you selected ($9.99/month or $99/year). You will be charged via the payment method you provided.

If you cancel before the trial ends, no charge is made and your access ends immediately. You can always re-subscribe at any time.
