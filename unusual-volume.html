What is unusual volume?

Unusual volume occurs when a stock trades significantly more shares than its historical average — often a sign of institutional activity, breaking news, or an earnings catalyst. A sudden spike in volume, especially when accompanied by a notable price move, can indicate the start of a meaningful trend.

This page ranks S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents by Vol/MA21 — today's volume divided by the 21-day moving average. A reading of 2.5× means the stock traded at 2.5 times its typical recent volume, which better isolates genuine spikes than a simple day-over-day comparison. The 1D VOL CHG% column shows the move versus the prior trading day's close for additional context.
