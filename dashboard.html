Metric Formula Description
1D Price Change (Close - Prev Close) / Prev Close Daily price momentum
1D Volume Change (Volume - Prev Volume) / Prev Volume Daily liquidity change
1D Price vs MA21 Close / MA21(Price) Current trend position vs baseline
1D Volume vs MA21 Volume / MA21(Volume) Current activity vs normal level
7D Max Price Change max((Close[t]-Close[t-1]) / Close[t-1]) Strongest daily price move in last 7 days
7D Max Volume Change max((Volume[t]-Volume[t-1]) / Volume[t-1]) Strongest volume spike in last 7 days
7D Max Price vs MA21 max(Close / MA21) Peak trend expansion in last 7 days
7D Max Volume vs MA21 max(Volume / MA21) Peak activity expansion in last 7 days

📊 MA21 Ratio Interpretation

Value Meaning

≈ 1.0 Neutral / baseline
