Signal Reference

Baizora scans S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents daily after market close. Each row reflects a stock's price and volume behavior relative to recent history using consistent statistical comparisons over rolling windows.

Signals are informational only — not buy, sell, or hold recommendations. Use the window buttons to switch timeframes (1D through 1Y). Hover any column header to see its description. Hover a ticker for fundamentals.
