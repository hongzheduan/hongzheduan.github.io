Signal Reference

Baizora scans S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents daily after market close. Each row reflects a stock's price and volume behavior relative to recent history using consistent statistical comparisons over rolling windows.

Signals are informational only — not buy, sell, or hold recommendations. Use the window buttons to switch timeframes (1D through 1Y). The 1D view shows today's 1-day metrics. All other windows show that window's total price change plus max single-day stats. Hover any column header to see its description. Hover a ticker for fundamentals.

◈   Scores (SCORES Tab)

Seven composite scores calculated daily from the same price and volume data. All scores are recalculated after every market close. Scores above 50 are highlighted green; below 50 are red. Vol Pressure uses 0 as its midpoint. Turn Score uses 50 / 25 as its signal thresholds.

ScoreCalculation & Interpretation
Baizora (0–100)Weighted composite of all five signals: RS 30% + Momentum 25% + Breakout 20% + Trend 15% + Vol Pressure 10%. Use as a quick-glance overall ranking. Sort descending to surface the strongest broad setups.
Turn (0–100)Weak-to-strong reversal signal: Breakout × (1 − RS/100) × VolPressure. Peaks when a historically weak stock (low RS) shows strong recent breakout action backed by buying volume. ▲ 50+ = strong signal; → 25–49 = emerging; below 25 = no signal. Drops to 0 when volume pressure is negative.
RS (1–99)Relative Strength — percentile rank across all 500+ stocks. Formula: 40% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 9M + 20% × 1Y price change, then ranked. 99 = top 1% of performers; 1 = bottom 1%.
Breakout (0–100)Three signals combined: price above MA21 (0–33 pts, scaled by distance above the average) + today's price gain (0–33 pts) + volume above MA21 (0–34 pts). 100 = all three firing at full strength simultaneously.
Momentum (0–100)Percentile rank of weighted cross-timeframe price change: 35% × 1M + 25% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 1Y. Favours stocks with consistent strength across every window, not just one-day pops. 100 = strongest sustained momentum in the universe.
Vol Pressure (−100 to 100)Accumulation vs distribution: (Vol/MA21 − 1) × price direction × 100. Positive = elevated volume on an up day (buying pressure). Negative = elevated volume on a down day (distribution). Near 0 = low volume or mixed signal.
Trend (0–100)Trend Consistency — percentage of the 6 timeframes (2W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 9M, 1Y) showing a positive price change. 100 = all 6 pointing up; 0 = all pointing down. Separates stocks in genuine sustained uptrends from choppy or reversing ones.
