Signal Reference

Baizora scans S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents daily after market close. Each row reflects a stock's price and volume behavior relative to recent history using consistent statistical comparisons over rolling windows.

Signals are informational only — not buy, sell, or hold recommendations. Use the window buttons to switch timeframes (1D through 1Y). The 1D view shows today's 1-day metrics. All other windows show that window's total price change plus max single-day stats. Hover any column header to see its description. Click a ticker to open its candlestick chart and fundamentals.

◈   Baizora Scores (BAIZORA Tab)

The Baizora composite-score family — each one blends the five raw signals below into a single 0–100 ranking, with a different emphasis. All are recalculated after every market close, and scores above 50 are highlighted green, below 50 red. This tab will grow as new variants are added.

ScoreCalculation & Interpretation
Baizora (0–100)The original weighted composite: RS 30% + Momentum 25% + Breakout 20% + Trend 15% + Vol Pressure 10%. Quick-glance overall ranking. Sort descending to surface the strongest broad setups. See the backtest ↗
Momentum+ (0–100)Alternate composite weighted toward trend/momentum: RS 40% + Momentum 35% + Breakout 15% + Trend 5% + Vol Pressure 5%. Backtested edge over Baizora is clearest among the very highest scorers (90+), on a thinner sample. See the backtest ↗
Persist (0–100)This stock's own trailing 21-session (~1 month) average Baizora score — a smoothed read on recent strength, not just today's snapshot. Feeds directly into Conviction below. Blank until ~1 month of history has accumulated. See the backtest ↗
Conviction (0–100)Geometric mean of today's Baizora score and this stock's own trailing 1-month average Baizora score — only high when a stock is both strong right now and has been strong consistently, not just a single spike day. Blank until ~1 month of history has accumulated for that ticker.
◈   Scores (SCORES Tab)

The five raw signals that feed every Baizora composite score above, shown individually for anyone who wants to see the components rather than just the blend.

ScoreCalculation & Interpretation
RS (1–99)Relative Strength — percentile rank across all 500+ stocks. Formula: 40% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 9M + 20% × 1Y price change, then ranked. 99 = top 1% of performers; 1 = bottom 1%.
Breakout (0–100)Three signals combined: price above MA21 (0–33 pts, scaled by distance above the average) + today's price gain (0–33 pts) + volume above MA21 (0–34 pts). 100 = all three firing at full strength simultaneously.
Momentum (0–100)Percentile rank of weighted cross-timeframe price change: 35% × 1M + 25% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 1Y. Favours stocks with consistent strength across every window, not just one-day pops. 100 = strongest sustained momentum in the universe.
Vol Pressure (−100 to 100)Accumulation vs distribution: (Vol/MA21 − 1) × price direction × 100. Positive = elevated volume on an up day (buying pressure). Negative = elevated volume on a down day (distribution). Near 0 = low volume or mixed signal.
Trend (0–100)Trend Consistency — percentage of the 6 timeframes (2W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 9M, 1Y) showing a positive price change. 100 = all 6 pointing up; 0 = all pointing down. Separates stocks in genuine sustained uptrends from choppy or reversing ones.

◈   Technicals (TECH Tab)
ColumnDescription
Dist SMA20 / Dist SMA200Absolute distance between the current price and the 20-day / 200-day moving average, as a percentage (SMAs computed server-side from up to 730 days of history). Green = price is above the average, red = price is below it. A stock trading close to (or just under) its SMA200 is often watched as a potential entry point, since the average tends to act as long-term support — not a guarantee, just a common technical read
MACDAbsolute distance between the MACD line and signal line (standard 12/26/9), as a percentage of price. Green = MACD above signal (bullish bias); red = below (bearish bias). Color flips exactly when the two lines cross
Dist POCAbsolute distance between the current price and the volume profile's point of control — the price level that traded the most volume over the last ~6 months — as a percentage. Green = price above that level, red = below. The point of control often acts as support/resistance or a magnet for price. Same calculation as the chart's Vol Profile overlay
