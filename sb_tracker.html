SB Tracker

S&P 500  ·  Nasdaq-100  ·  Updated each trading day

Each trading day, two steps. First, every S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 stock whose close rose on each of the last 2 trading days. Then those stocks are sorted by 1-year volatility (the annualized standard deviation of daily returns), highest first, and the top ten are listed. The list is a fixed rule applied to scanner data, with no human selection.

In a one-year backtest (October 2025 to October 2026, current index members, 7-day returns), this rule averaged +0.9% per list (median +0.6%) vs SPY +0.4% (median +0.2%), was ahead of SPY in 51% of lists, and its worst list lost 20.3%. It trailed SPY in the second half of that year, and the edge is small enough to be chance. Past results do not guarantee future results.

Returns are price only, from the list date's close to the close 7 trading days later, adjusted for splits, before dividends, taxes and trading costs. "Peak in 7 days" is each stock's highest intraday price in the 7 trading days after the list date (so far, for younger lists), with its % above the list-date close and the date it happened (day N = Nth trading day after the list date). Lists younger than 7 trading days show their return so far, marked "day 3 of 7", and are left out of the summary until they finish. "List mean" is the average of the listed stocks' returns (up to ten); SPY is the S&P 500 ETF over the same span. Lists marked † were rebuilt on October 28, 2026 from each day's published scanner data; lists after October 28, 2026 are recorded each evening. Until October 28, 2026 this tracker listed Technology stocks with product-launch news; that rule continues on the Custom Tracker. Volatility uses daily closes adjusted for dividends. Rankings are mechanical and change daily. For informational purposes only. Not investment advice.
