◈   Scores (SCORES Tab)
ColumnDescription
RS (1–99)Relative Strength — percentile rank across all 500+ stocks. Formula: 40% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 9M + 20% × 1Y price change. 99 = top 1%; 1 = bottom 1%.
Breakout (0–100)Price above MA21 (0–33 pts) + today's price gain (0–33 pts) + volume above MA21 (0–34 pts). 100 = all three signals at full strength.
Momentum (0–100)Percentile rank of weighted cross-timeframe price change: 35% × 1M + 25% × 3M + 20% × 6M + 20% × 1Y. Favours stocks with consistent strength across every window.
Vol Pressure (±100)Accumulation vs distribution. Positive = elevated volume on an up day (buying pressure). Negative = elevated volume on a down day (selling pressure).
Trend (0–100)Percentage of timeframes (2W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 9M, 1Y) showing positive price change. 100 = all 6 timeframes pointing up.
