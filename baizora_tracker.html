Baizora Tracker

S&P 500  ·  Nasdaq-100  ·  Updated each trading day

Each trading day, the ten S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 stocks with the largest 3-month price change in the price & volume scanner. The rank score is that change as a percentile among all eligible stocks that day; ties go to the higher Baizora Score. The list is a fixed rule applied to scanner data, with no human selection.

In a one-year test (October 2025 to October 2026), the ten stocks with the largest 3-month gains had a higher 13-day return than the ten with the highest BaizConviction, a difference too small to be statistically reliable. Ranking by recent gains favors stocks that have already run up, and such lists can fall sharply when a trend turns. Past results do not guarantee future results.
